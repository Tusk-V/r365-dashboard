# Andy's Dashboard — Features & Benefits

*One operations platform for every Andy's location. Built for owners and
operators, not IT departments.*

---

## The short version

Andy's Dashboard pulls your Restaurant365, scheduling, and POS data together
every morning and turns it into something you can actually act on: a live
picture of every store, an AI-written debrief in your inbox, a forecast that
sharpens itself over time, and labor numbers that tell you whether you're making
money or leaking it. It runs ~20 locations across four markets today and is
already in daily use.

The point isn't more reports. It's fewer decisions left to guesswork.

---

## What it does for you

### See every store before your coffee's cold
A single live dashboard covers daily sales, weekly sales, labor, overtime,
forecasting, scheduled-today, paid-outs, call-offs, clockouts, and the manager
logbook. It refreshes on its own and works on your phone, so the whole portfolio
is one glance away — from the office or the parking lot.
**Benefit:** problems surface while you can still fix them, not in next week's report.

### A written debrief in your inbox by 7 AM
Every morning the system reads the prior day's numbers and writes a plain-English
debrief — wins, what needs attention, and store-by-store notes — and emails it to
the team. The weekly version lands on Mondays.
**Benefit:** the performance review that used to need an analyst now writes itself, in a consistent, constructive voice, every single day.

### A forecast that gets smarter on its own
The sales model learns from your own history. It leans on last week as the
baseline, factors in weather, and knows the difference between a normal Saturday
and a holiday, spring break, or the day after one. It re-tunes itself with each
daily run.
**Benefit:** you staff and order against a number you can trust — and it keeps improving without anyone maintaining it.

### Labor numbers that protect your margin
The dashboard compares actual hours against what management scheduled and against
an optimal benchmark, then flags when extra labor cost didn't buy enough sales to
justify it. It even accounts for auto-clockouts, so your labor picture is honest
rather than alarmist.
**Benefit:** you see whether every scheduled hour earned its keep — and catch overspend before it compounds across stores.

### P&L without the spreadsheet wrestling
Drop in one Excel export from R365 and all three views populate at once — current
vs. prior period, period vs. YTD, and YTD vs. prior YTD. Flip between a single
store and a whole market with one toggle.
**Benefit:** period and year-to-date P&L, rolled up by market or drilled to one store, in seconds instead of an afternoon.

### Manager bonuses, calculated and consistent
Bonus math runs off the same trusted numbers, so payouts are transparent and the
rules apply the same way to everyone.
**Benefit:** no manual spreadsheets, no disputes over how a number was reached.

### Recaps that reach the right manager
Each manager gets a recap for their own store, driven by an editable roster, with
an exception gate so only what actually needs attention gets escalated.
**Benefit:** accountability lands where it belongs without burying anyone in noise.

### Team communication in the same place as the data
A built-in messaging system gives you company-wide, market, and per-store
channels, a managers-only space, and access for hourly associates — with push
notifications so the right message reaches the right people fast.
**Benefit:** operational conversations happen next to the numbers that prompted them, not scattered across texts and group chats.

### The right people see the right data
Access is tied to role: owners and field managers see everything, store managers
see their stores, and associates get chat without the financials — enforced on
the server, not just hidden in the interface. Sign-in is locked to your company
Google accounts.
**Benefit:** sensitive numbers stay with the people who should have them, by default.

### It runs itself every morning
Data flows in automatically from R365 and your sheets on a fixed early-morning
schedule, with no button to push and no server to babysit. It's hosted in the
cloud and built to scale across markets.
**Benefit:** the information is just there when you wake up — no IT team, no manual pulls, no morning scramble.
